Who We Are

Le_gendary supplies is a regional provider of medical and laboratory equipment, consumables and support services. We source reliable products from trusted manufacturers and deliver tailored solutions for clinics, hospitals and diagnostic labs.

Founded to improve access to quality healthcare equipment, we combine product supply, installation and after-sales service to maximise uptime and value for our customers.

Our Mission & Values

  • Mission: Enable better patient care through dependable equipment and responsive service.
  • Values: Quality, Integrity, Customer-focus, Continuous improvement.
  • Vision: To be the preferred partner for medical and laboratory procurement in the region.

Services We Provide

  • Supply of medical devices, diagnostics and laboratory instruments
  • Installation, commissioning and user training
  • Preventive maintenance, repairs and spare parts
  • Project procurement and turnkey lab fit-outs
