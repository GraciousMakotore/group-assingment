How to Order

  1. Browse our product catalog on the Products page or request a tailored quote.
  2. Request a quote online or email sales@le_gendary.supplies with product codes and delivery details.
  3. Receive a proforma invoice — confirm order by email or purchase order.
  4. We arrange shipping and installation; payment terms are shown on the invoice.
